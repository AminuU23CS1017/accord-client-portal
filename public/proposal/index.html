01 / From ambition to evidence

A plan you can
hold me to.

Accord is my starting concept. A working demo shows what I can build; the next step is to learn whether small businesses need it enough to use it and pay for it.

DAYS 01–14 / DISCOVER

Find the right problem.

Speak to at least 10 potential users. Learn how small businesses prepare quotes today, where they lose time, and whether this prototype solves a problem worth paying for.

Deliverable: interview notes, one chosen problem, and a simple product brief.
DAYS 15–45 / BUILD

Make the smallest useful version.

Improve the quote-builder prototype around what users actually need. Test it with five people and use their feedback to refine the product.

Deliverable: a working demo and feedback from five testers.
DAYS 46–90 / VALIDATE

Ask people to pay.

Offer the product to potential buyers. Aim for three paying customers, record what people accept or reject, and review whether to continue.

Deliverable: sales evidence, costs, lessons, and a next-step recommendation.

These are proposed targets, not results already achieved. The 90-day clock starts once we agree on the plan.

04 / The questions that matter

Let’s talk about the risk.

What exactly are you building?

Accord is an early quote builder for freelancers and small service businesses. It already supports customer details, itemized services, automatic totals, and printable quotes. The first 14 days will test whether this is the right product and audience to pursue.

What if nobody pays?

Then we review the evidence before spending further. I’ll report what we tried, what people said, and whether a smaller change is worth testing. The plan allows us to stop; it does not assume success.

Is this a loan, a gift, or an investment?

That is something we need to agree on before funding starts. This proposal does not promise repayment, equity, or a financial return. The arrangement and expectations should be written down together.

How will we decide on the funding?

Let’s first review the idea, the working demo, and your feedback. Then we can agree on the scope, actual costs, and support for the first milestone. Any later funding would depend on progress and your agreement.
